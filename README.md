# CRM Customer Analytics| Power BI

## Project Overview
This project demonstrates how customer relationships management (CRM) data can be analyzed and visualized to support data driven business decision.
Using a synthetic dataset of 16 customers, I developed a Power BI dashboard to explore customer segmentation, revenue, performance and customer retention risk.
The object is to identify customer groups that may require additional attention and translate data into actionable business insights.


## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel 
- Power BI 
- DAX


## Key Performance Indicators 
| KPI | Result |
|-----|-----|
| Total Customers | 16 |
| Total Revenue | 9,916 € |
| Average Revenue per Customer | 619,69 € |
| At-Risk Customers | 6 |
| At Risk Customer Rate | 37.5 % |


## Key Insights 
- 37.5 % of customers are classified as at risk.
- The Standard segment has a higher at risk rate (45.5 %) than the Premium segment (20 %).
- The Standard segment could be prioritized for customer retention activities. 
- Further analysis would be required to understand the underlying causes of customer risk.

## Business Recommendations 
- Prioritized follow-up activities for at-risk customers.
- Monitor customer status and revenue KPIs regularly.
- Investigate potential reasons for customer inactivity.
- Develop targeted retention strategies for different customer segments.
